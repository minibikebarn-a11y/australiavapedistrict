About

Disposables, devices, and doing it properly.

Disposable vapes are single-use devices, pre-filled and pre-charged, built to be used until empty and then replaced — no refilling, no charging, no maintenance. Refillable devices (pod kits and mods) trade that convenience for control: you choose the e-liquid, manage the coil, and get more use out of one device over time. Both have a place depending on how you vape.

Safety comes down to a few fundamentals: buy from a source that discloses what's actually in the product, store devices away from heat and out of reach of children and pets, stop using a device if it leaks, overheats, or tastes burnt, and never modify a device beyond what the manufacturer intends. All products listed here are intended for adults only.
