Privacy Policy

This policy explains how australiavapedistrict ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use this website and place an order.

Information we collect

When you place an order, we collect the details you provide at checkout: name, contact details, shipping and billing address, order notes, and your chosen payment method. When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. We do not collect or store cryptocurrency wallet private keys, bank login credentials, or card numbers — payment is completed directly between you and the relevant payment channel.

How we use your information

We use your information to process and ship your order, communicate with you about order status, respond to enquiries through email or live chat, and improve the website. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Payment information

For cryptocurrency payments, we generate a payment address and amount specific to your order. For bank transfer or PayID, payment details are provided directly by a live agent rather than displayed on the website, and are not stored in our systems.

Data storage and security

Order and contact information is stored securely and access is limited to what's needed to fulfil your order. We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

Cookies

This website may use cookies to remember items in your cart and improve site performance. You can disable cookies in your browser, though some features (like the shopping cart) may not work correctly without them.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us using the details on our Contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@australiavapedistrict.com or 261 Church St, Parramatta NSW 2150, Australia.
